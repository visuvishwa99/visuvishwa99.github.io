Reporting, streaming & observability

Lexia Learning

Senior Data Engineer (Consultant, Remote)

Faster reporting across education products

The challenge
Tableau workbooks queried MySQL directly, while Core5, PowerUp, and LETRS data needed to support district, school, and educator reporting for Customer Data 360.
My approach
I moved reporting logic into dbt marts on Redshift and built an event-ingestion path that handles replayed student activity.
What changed
Report-processing time fell by 70%. The new analytical layer supported workbook migration from direct MySQL connections, alongside deduplicated student-activity data for downstream reporting.

Lakehouse modernization & performance

CAVA

Senior Data Engineer (Consultant)

Scaling restaurant analytics while reducing cost

The challenge
Store analytics had to bring together historical archives, POS, workforce, and finance feeds while CAVA expanded from 341 to 398 restaurants.
My approach
I consolidated the feeds in Delta Lake, redesigned data layouts, and refined incremental processing. Metadata-driven ingestion, schema checks, and key-based deduplication supported the growing reporting footprint.
What changed
Processing time fell by 30%, platform costs by 20%, and query-execution time by 25%. Validated datasets also supported preparation for planned AI-driven personalization.

Cloud migration & analytics modeling

Calix

Senior Data Engineer (Consultant, Remote)

Turning 250 TB of Oracle history into cloud-accessible data

The challenge
Historical data sat in on-premises Oracle, while reporting needed connected views of Oracle Fusion orders and service contracts.
My approach
I moved the historical data into Azure and modeled order and service-contract relationships in Snowflake and dbt, using shared dimensions to connect the reporting views.
What changed
Migrated 250 TB of historical data into cloud storage and delivered Power BI reporting marts that connected order and contract records through shared dimensions.

Configuration-driven pipeline design

Freddie Mac

AWS Data Engineer (Consultant, Remote)

Onboarding datasets without changing core code

The challenge
Denormalized loan-evaluation documents needed to become usable reporting datasets without losing the relationships between parent and child records.
My approach
I built a reusable PySpark framework with configurable dataset mappings and runtime parameters. The transformation preserved identifiers, with counts and relationships validated across processing layers.
What changed
Configuration replaced core-code changes for dataset onboarding. Eight staging datasets supported delivery of 18 curated datasets for research and enterprise reporting.

Credit-line batch processing & audit

American Express

Big Data Engineer II (Consultant)

Auditable credit-line processing at daily batch scale

The challenge
Approximately 25 TB of daily risk and behavioral input had to move through eligibility and decisioning while keeping published offers traceable to their source.
My approach
I automated distinct feature, eligibility, decisioning, and fulfillment stages, and standardized validation and audit workflows for international credit-line offers.
What changed
The daily batch produced audited downstream outputs, with offers across Japan and Europe linked back to source attributes and decision results.
